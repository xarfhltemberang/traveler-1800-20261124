One sentence, at most 200 characters, explaining the modern internet to a
time traveler who has just stepped out of the year 1800 — in words and
pictures a person of that year could actually hold in their head: a library,
a post office, a road, a letter, a book.

> Imagine every library and post office in the world joined by one road, so any man may read any book or send any letter to any door in a breath, without leaving his chair.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt` (170 characters, one sentence,
no word coined after 1800).
